Maintenance

RestartReboot the module. Settings are kept.
Rescue mode Restart once with default settings, to recover a module you can't reach anymore (wrong WiFi settings, faulty configuration).
  • On this restart only, the saved settings are not loaded: the module opens its own WiFi network (default access point password) and answers at 192.168.4.1.
  • Saved settings are not erased: the next normal restart brings them back, unless you save new settings while in rescue mode.
  • Without this page, press the rescue button in the first seconds after powering on the module to get the same result (on devices that have one).
